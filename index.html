Our mission

We build oil and gas field equipment that holds up to the work it's asked to do. That means engineering it with the same care whether the job is a two-wire harness or a full skid rebuild — laid out clean, labeled clearly, and tested before it leaves the shop.

What we build

Four kinds of work make up most of what comes through the shop.

Control panels

Custom-fabricated panels — fuse blocks, switches, meters, and terminal strips laid out and labeled to spec.

Depth & encoders

Splitter boxes, rotary encoders, and measuring-head electronics, reconditioned or built new.

Harnesses & breakouts

Hand-terminated cable assemblies and breakout boxes built to route cleanly through a rig or skid.

Testing & repair

Continuity and voltage checks on everything that ships, plus fault-finding on existing systems.

Services

What we build, wire, and fix — from a single breakout box to a full skid.

01 — PANELS

Custom control panel fabrication

We design and build control panels from the enclosure up — fuse blocks, switches, meters, and terminal blocks laid out, labeled, and dressed for a panel that's easy to service later.

  • Enclosure layout and drilling
  • Labeled terminal strips
  • Panel meters and switching
  • Built to your spec, or ours
02 — DEPTH & ENCODERS

Depth measurement & encoder systems

Splitter boxes, rotary encoders, and measuring-head electronics for depth and tension systems — reconditioned or built new. We've worked on gear from Century Geophysical, Linewise, and other wireline equipment makers.

  • Encoder reconditioning and bench testing
  • Depth splitter boxes
  • Tension and slip-ring wiring
  • Compatibility with common logging systems
03 — HARNESSES

Custom cable & harness assemblies

Hand-terminated harnesses and breakout boxes, built to route power, signal, and ground cleanly through a rig or skid.

  • Multi-pin connector termination
  • Braided and armored cable assemblies
  • Labeled breakout boxes
  • Solder and crimp termination
04 — SKIDS & VEHICLES

Skid & vehicle electrical builds

Full electrical builds for skids, service trucks, and logging units — from the main switching hub down to the last light switch.

  • Main switching and fuse hubs
  • Interior and exterior lighting circuits
  • Camera and alarm wiring
  • Shop / rig power switching
05 — TESTING

Testing, diagnostics & repair

Every circuit gets checked before it ships. And if something in the field stops working, we can track it down and fix it.

  • Continuity and voltage testing
  • Fault-finding on existing systems
  • Component-level repair
  • Bench testing before install

About Cornerstone Pro

Cornerstone Pro is a Calgary-based shop that designs, builds, and repairs the electrical systems oil and gas field equipment depends on — control panels, depth and encoder systems, wiring harnesses, and full skid builds for wireline and logging operations.

Every panel starts on the bench: laid out, labeled, and wired by hand, then tested before it goes anywhere near a rig. That's the standard whether the job is a small breakout box or a complete switching hub.

We're based in Calgary, Alberta, and work with operators and service companies across the field.

Get in touch
